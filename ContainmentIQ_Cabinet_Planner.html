Measurements + provenance

Checking this records your assertion; it does not certify fit or validate the model automatically. Editing geometry clears the assertion.

Cabinet baseline: newer Thermo Scientific 1300 Series A2 manual 7021355 Rev. 22 and product brochure, model 1377. Interior 1800 W × 630 D × 780 H mm. Working sash opening 254 mm; full loading opening 535 mm. Older manual 7011355 Rev. 20 lists 627 mm interior depth and a 500 mm loading opening. Confirm the actual nameplate, revision and measurements.

Rover baseline: catalog 194 L × 168 W × 100 H mm. The official STL has an approximate 194.0 × 172.4 × 99.9 mm bounding box; published drawings may differ. Measure the actual wheels and hardware before using a footprint. Probe heights, exclusions, assembly and timing defaults are illustrative.

Manufacturer references (online): newer operating manual · cabinet brochure · older operating manual · WAVE ROVER wiki + drawings.

Test readiness
  1. Confirm the cabinet nameplate and usable deck dimensions with a tape or caliper.
  2. Build and measure a cabinet-sized mount. Check loading through the sash and clearance at every height.
  3. Confirm probe travel, homing, limit stops and cable management.
  4. Test the rover on an open bench. Check stop, direction and repeatability before cabinet work.
  5. Add external position feedback and a verified motion controller before executing coordinate routes. Stock WAVE ROVER has no wheel encoders; PWM values are not measured speed.
